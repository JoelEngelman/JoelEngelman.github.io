Joel Engelman

Joel Engelman is a passionate singer, songwriter, rapper and producer. Growing up with a stutter made singing especially challenging, but it never stopped him from pursuing the dream of making music.

Born in Australia and raised in Fiji, Joel was surrounded by creativity from a young age. Inspired by family, music and art, he now writes, records, produces and performs his own music while continuing to learn and create.
