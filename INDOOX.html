Joel Engelman

Joel Engelman is a passionate singer, songwriter, rapper and producer. Despite growing up with a stutter, Joel never let it stop him from pursuing the dream of creating music.

Born in Australia and raised in Fiji, Joel has been surrounded by music, creativity and inspiration from a young age. Today he writes, records, produces and performs his own music while continually expanding his skills as both an artist and creator.
