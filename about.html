My Story

Joel Engelman is a passionate singer, songwriter, rapper, and producer. At a young age, Joel found out he had a stutter, which made it very hard and difficult for anyone to sing, especially because unlike most people who stutter, Joel found the he didnt stutter less when he sang, in fact, in some cases, he'd stutter even more, but that didn't stop him from doing what he had always dreamt of doing. Making music. Joel was born in Australia, and raised in Fiji. Joel grew up around arts and music. Both of his parents were doctors, though Joel's dad was a DJ and played many instruments, and Joel's mother was an amazing artist. Joel has been inspired by those around him from a very young age. Joel plays the piano, the drums, he DJ's (taught from his father), sings, writes, produces, and even coded this website!
