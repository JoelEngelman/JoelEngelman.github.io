Joel Engelman

Joel Engelman is a passionate singer, songwriter, rapper, and producer. At a young age, Joel found out he had a stutter, which made it very hard and difficult to sing. Unlike many people who stutter, Joel found that singing wasn't always easier—in some cases he would stutter even more—but that never stopped him from chasing his dream of making music.

Joel was born in Australia and raised in Fiji. Growing up around music and the arts, he developed a passion for creativity from an early age. His father enjoyed DJing and playing musical instruments, while his mother is an accomplished artist.

Inspired by the people around him from a very young age, Joel continued developing his musical abilities. Today he plays the piano, the drums, DJs, sings, writes songs, produces music, and even coded this very website from scratch.
