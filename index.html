Joel Engelman

Joel Engelman is a passionate singer, songwriter, rapper, and producer. At a young age, Joel found out he had a stutter, which made it difficult to sing. Unlike many people who stutter, Joel found that singing wasn't always easier for him—in some cases, he would stutter even more. Despite those challenges, he continued pursuing what he had always dreamed of doing: making music.

Joel was born in Australia and raised in Fiji. Growing up surrounded by creativity, he developed a love for music and the arts from an early age. His father, alongside his medical career, enjoyed DJing and playing musical instruments, while his mother is an accomplished artist.

Inspired by the people around him, Joel continued developing his musical abilities. Today he plays the piano and drums, DJs, sings, writes songs, produces music, and even coded this website himself.
